Committee Scope

Advancing Smart
Aerospace Systems

The AIAA Adaptive Structures Technical Committee focuses on autonomous structural systems incorporating sensors, processors, and actuators to enable adaptation to changing environmental conditions.

Our research enhances safety, stability, vibration damping, acoustic noise suppression, aerodynamic performance, pointing accuracy, load redistribution, damage response, and structural integrity across aerospace platforms.
